Slowpoke Derby Support

The Grand Gastropod Gala: only the slowest may enter.

Need help, found a bug, or have an idea?

Open an issue on GitHub. Please include your phone or tablet model, the stage number, and what happened.

How to play

  • Hold CREEP to move. Keep the pace needle in the green sweet spot. Go too fast for too long and the judges send you back to the last checkpoint.
  • Zen fills while you stay in the sweet spot. A full Zen bar shields you from one hit.
  • Tap TUCK just before a robin, boot, acorn or flood strikes. Tucking just in time is a Perfect tuck and earns a bonus dewdrop.
  • Gates (ant crossings and rake sweeps): wait for the green lamp.
  • Hills use slime. When it runs out, let go and rest to refill it.
  • Nap leaves: stop on one to fill Zen quickly.

Questions

How do I replay a stage or start over?

On the title screen, tap Stages. Pick any stage you’ve reached, or tap “Start over from Stage 1”. Your dewdrops, upgrades and hats are kept.

How do I erase everything?

Title screen › Settings › Reset progress. This clears stages, dewdrops, upgrades and hats. Settings stay.

The races are too hard (or too easy)

Choose Gentle, Normal or Hard on the title screen. Gentle widens the sweet spot and gives longer warnings. Hard ends the race on the first bonk and pays 50% more dewdrops. Settings also has auto-tuck for one-button play.

How do I beat Marmalade the Cat?

She swipes three times. Stay tucked through each swipe, or wait outside her shadow until she gets bored and wanders off.

The game feels slow on my phone

The game picks lighter graphics automatically on slower phones. Reduce motion in Settings also turns off screen shake.

Does the game need the internet or collect data?

No. It plays fully offline, has no ads or in-app purchases, and collects no data. See the privacy policy.
