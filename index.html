Slowpoke Derby Privacy Policy

Effective October 31, 2026 · Applies to Slowpoke Derby for iPhone, iPad and Android

Support and FAQ
The short version: Slowpoke Derby collects no data.
  • No account and no sign-in.
  • We don’t collect, receive, sell or share any information about you. The game has no servers.
  • No ads, no analytics, no tracking and no in-app purchases.
  • The game plays fully offline.

What the game stores, and where

To remember your progress, the game keeps one small save file in its own storage on your device. It contains:

  • Your progress: the next stage, the furthest stage reached, best times, ribbons and story progress.
  • Your dewdrops, upgrades, hats and chosen racer.
  • Your settings, such as sound, music, vibration, difficulty and graphics quality.

This file never leaves your device and we cannot see it. You can clear it at any time in Settings › Reset progress. Deleting the app deletes it too.

Permissions

On Android the game asks only to use vibration, for gentle haptic feedback during races (you can turn it off in Settings). On iPhone and iPad it asks for no permissions. The game never uses your camera, microphone, location, contacts or photos.

Backups

If you use iCloud Backup (iPhone and iPad) or Google’s device backup (Android), your phone may include the save file in its backup. These backups are run by Apple or Google under their privacy policies, in your own account. We can’t access them.

What we don’t do

  • No advertising, advertising IDs, or tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics, crash reporting or usage statistics.
  • No third-party SDKs that collect data, and no data sales.
  • No chat, sharing or contact with other players.

Children

Slowpoke Derby is a general-audience game that is safe for all ages. Because it does not collect personal information from anyone, it does not collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If a future version adds a feature that works differently, this page will be updated before that version is released, with a new effective date.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Open an issue on GitHub.
